# Pursuit15: an actual, bounded encounter

The previous optional activation chose the first scrap spot over 18 metres from everyone. On real generated layouts that spot could be hidden beyond several walls; the Warden never witnessed the initiator, so an apparently successful hide produced no recording. The activation now runs one bounded scan (96 candidates maximum) around the initiator, preferring 12 metres and falling back to 10, 8 and 16. Every candidate requires a real nav path, actual floor, head LOS and actual creature perception, body clearance, at least seven metres from every living crewmate and no entrance/ship safe zone. Excessively circuitous paths are rejected. No suitable position means localized feedback and no consumed landing attempt. The warning still gives 2.2 seconds before movement; there is no adjacent spawn or instant strike.

Search now reacts to real visual reacquisition by returning to chase, remembering the newly seen location and restarting the subsequent unseen search. Winning still requires genuine prior sight during warning/chase/windup, then six uninterrupted seconds without sight. A visible twelve-second chase timeout cannot award salvage. Total attempt time is capped at 35 seconds, including stunned time; stuns fail the optional objective. Both normal module ticks and standalone AI use a bounded clock. The replicated host clock also survives migration. Hit, death/downing and boss/bonus-mission conflicts remain failure conditions. Success/failure produces one short localized message. A successful attempt unlocks one physical recording worth 85 salvage at the first alcove, with ordinary carrying/sale rules and no free XP.

Warden-specific door handling prevents generic nav following from opening doors early or behind it. A genuinely forward closed door costs 1.8 seconds before opening. Movement probes now cover the centre and both body edges at two heights, so shelter panels obstruct its body instead of only its centre. This adds at most six short physics queries per moving Warden tick; there is at most one opt-in Warden per landing. Candidate A* and clearance work occur only on activation, never per frame.

Validation in `tools/harness/escape14.test.mjs` uses real factory seed 1235, actual Rapier floor/wall queries, a real HostCreature and CreatureManager.hostUpdate with the actual nav follower and perception. It activates from the first real shelter without forcing AI state, ticks the warning and genuine moving chase, closes a real physical door in its forward route and verifies the full pause/open window, then moves the player to actual panel cover and observes ordinary unseen-search success and removal. Separate actual-AI regressions cover fresh search after reacquisition, never-seen failure, uninterrupted visible time cap, all failure gates and exactly one physical claim. Existing physical shelter/membership/cleanup tests remain. The test positions the player for deterministic navigation evidence; it does not claim human difficulty or complete map-wide clearance. Browser QA owns physical interaction and crew replication verification.
