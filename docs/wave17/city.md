# Wave17 optional city courier

The existing moving neutral citizens now provide an optional physical delivery after the first field day (`run.day > 1`) or quota index 1. Fresh day-one hub prompts remain fleet-focused. The original curated safe walking routes, alien silhouettes and survey/barter cases remain.

Citizen 0 is the mint-marked dispatcher; citizen 1 is the amber-marked recipient across the plaza. E at the dispatcher issues one identifiable sealed `courier17` parcel into the native item system. Players pick it up, drop it, store it in a bag or pass it between crew members through ordinary controls. E at the recipient consumes that exact parcel and pays 12 native crew credits. City role prompts and short dialogue, item description and departure explanation support EN/TR/RU. Two small role markers follow the actual NPC models; they add no lights or per-frame geometry.

The host accepts `l13req` operations `dispatch`/`deliver`, actor ID and current map token. It rejects dead/downed/in-ship players, remote requests and blocked STATIC/DOOR lines of sight. Delivery requires the exact host-recorded native parcel type/ID, held state and requester custody; bag custody is accepted. Client amount and item fields are ignored. The shared `run.life13.mission.paid` slot closes courier, survey and barter for the whole crew/day. State is reserved before network callbacks, preventing repeated requests from duplicating parcels or rewards.

Departure or day change returns only the recorded temporary parcel, cancels an unfinished courier contract and releases its unpaid shared slot. A localized departure message explains this; no new mandatory objective/HUD panel remains stranded. Completed payment remains finite for that day. City routes and all four vessel paths were not modified.

Model geometry is owned by the native item lifecycle; parcel materials use the existing render cache. NPC role-marker geometry/materials are explicitly disposed before their citizen model. No extra currency, NPC combat, wall clock progression or light source is added.

Validation: `source /workspace/.tfg-tools/activate.sh; node tools/harness/life13.test.mjs` executes the actual installed module and registered host handler. Checks cover fresh-hub gating, E dispatch, LOS/proximity rejection, repeated issuance, exact custody, peer delivery from bag, malicious client reward fields, single payment, shared city-to-moon and survey-to-city budget, day reset and exact temporary-item removal on unload. Existing company NPC unload lifecycle check also passes.
