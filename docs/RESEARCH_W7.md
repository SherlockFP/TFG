# RESEARCH W7: what makes co-op horror / extraction / "friendslop" games work (and fail)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked for most domains, so claims come from WebSearch result summaries of the URLs cited. Items marked `[inference]` are my synthesis, not a sourced claim. Dredge is not sourced (no search result covered it); Inscryption is.

## 1. Per-game breakdown

### Lethal Company (Zeekerss, 2023, $10, ~10M sold)
Why it works
- Diegetic proximity voice turns communication into a managed risk: too far and you cannot hear allies, tunnels echo, a friend's voice cuts off mid-sentence when they die. https://www.gamedesignlibrary.com/post/proximity-chat-changes-the-game-how-lethal-company-s-game-design-innovated-multiplayer-horror-games
- Clear, cruel loop: scavenge scrap, hit a rising quota, or get ejected into space. Loot is only banked if the carrier survives, so one death shakes the whole crew. https://lethalcompanyreview.com/lethal-company-review-a-masterclass-in-chaotic-co-op-horror/
- Asymmetric roles create engagement for the "ops" player on the ship (doors, turrets, radar), who is the team's lifeline. https://lethalcompanyreview.com/lethal-company-review-a-masterclass-in-chaotic-co-op-horror/
- Monsters are simple rule-based forcing functions (Coil-Head forces you to look at it and turn your back on everything else). https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-lethal-company-sold-10-million-copies
- Highly watchable: emergent scary/funny moments fed Twitch/YouTube; $10 price, solo dev, "true virality" comparable to Among Us. https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/lethal-company-sold-an-estimated-10-million-copies , https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-can-we-learn-from-lethal-companys
Weaknesses
- Repetitive interiors, few monsters, thin late game; default game "boring" without mods, modded lobbies split the playerbase. https://www.zleague.gg/theportal/lethal-company-gamers-speak-out-on-the-top-issues/
- Peak ~240k CCU, down to ~5k by 2025 (novelty decay). https://www.avclub.com/not-every-co-op-game-is-friendslop (via search summary)

### R.E.P.O. (Semiwork, 2025, $10, 18.5M copies in 2025)
Why it works
- Physics-based grabbing: items have weight, fragility and value, so carrying is a skill and a comedy engine ("never occurred to me Lethal Company was missing physics"). https://gamerant.com/co-op-horror-game-like-lethal-company-hit-steam-pc-repo/
- Heavy items need teamwork (drag, co-lift); monsters are also physics-affected, making them funny as well as scary. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/ , https://earlygame.com/guides/gaming/how-to-handle-every-monster-in-r-e-p-o
- Monster sensory diversity (blind Huntsman hunts by sound; Robe stalks unseen), forcing different behaviour per enemy. https://repo-2025horror.fandom.com/wiki/Huntsman
- Hub-and-upgrade loop between runs, up to 6 players, 96% positive; won Golden Joysticks Best Early Access 2025. https://www.thegamer.com/is-repo-worth-it/
- Dev says it began as a singleplayer cleaning game, not a Lethal clone, i.e. it grew from its own core verb (grab/carry physics). https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/repo-dev-says-it-wasnt-actually-inspired-by-lethal-company-and-started-as-a-singleplayer-cleaning-game-it-was-nice-but-far-from-what-repo-is-now/
Weaknesses
- Physics is the most technically fragile part (unpredictable collisions); limited maps/objective variety over long play. https://gamerant.com/co-op-horror-game-like-lethal-company-hit-steam-pc-repo/

### Content Warning (Landfall, 2024)
Why it works
- The "film to go viral" verb: camera is the tool, views/money scale with monster danger, on-screen presence and interaction, so players *perform* horror. https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/content-warning-a-free-co-op-horror-game-where-you-go-viral-on-spooktube-or-die-trying-is-rapidly-climbing-the-steam-charts/
- Camera has limited storage/battery; selfie mode while fleeing; constant tension between surviving and making entertaining footage. https://game8.co/articles/reviews/content-warning-review
- Meta-loop: players produce shareable clips, marketing itself. 24h free giveaway gave 6.2M owners, 200k CCU; then priced and still sold 2.2M in 2 months. https://www.gamesradar.com/a-24-hour-free-giveaway-sent-steams-new-viral-co-op-mega-hit-to-the-moon-with-62-million-owners-after-its-first-day/ , https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/turns-out-you-can-give-away-66-million-copies-of-your-game-add-a-price-later-and-still-sell-22-million-more-in-just-2-months/
- Short day-based sessions with a quota of views.
Weaknesses
- Many monsters have "just avoid" counterplay; views quota scales linearly so runs feel the same; players can metagame with tiny clips and leave others useless; few maps, slow updates. https://steamcommunity.com/app/2881650/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated , https://blueandqueenie.com/content-warning-review/

### Phasmophobia (Kinetic Games, forerunner of the genre)
Why it works
- A deduction loop: gather behaviour/evidence clues, identify ghost among many types, get out, get paid. https://gamecritics.com/jason-ricci/phasmophobia-review-ready/
- Ghost variety with distinct personalities gives replay in the same houses; voice recognition and proximity voice make talking to the ghost part of play. https://gamecritics.com/jason-ricci/phasmophobia-review-ready/
- Hunt phase is a hard tension spike after a long calm investigation (pacing contrast).
- Highest retention in the friendslop family (D30 ~5.3% vs ~3% average). https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025
Weaknesses
- Repetitive same-map loop; trial-and-error waiting 30-40 minutes for one piece of evidence; "looping" exploit around furniture breaks immersion. https://steamcommunity.com/app/739630/discussions/0/4626979317219344385/ , https://steamcommunity.com/app/739630/discussions/0/694249895115101629/

### Deep Rock Galactic (Ghost Ship)
Why it works
- Four classes, none mandatory, each adds a role (Scout mobility, Gunner protection/revives, Engineer platforms/turrets, Driller routes and swarm-clearing): most failed runs are failures of mutual support. https://jeu.video/en/guide/deep-rock-galactic-classes-coop-en
- Destructible procedural caves; mining and combat alternate: quiet exploration then swarm defence. https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2020/05/deep-rock-galactic-review-an-otherworldly-co-op-experience/
- 10-40 minute missions; difficulty scales with group size; short clear objectives. https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2020/05/deep-rock-galactic-review-an-otherworldly-co-op-experience/
- Social glue: "Rock and Stone" call-and-response, safe-to-jump-in ethos (accessibility).
Weakness
- It is a shooter-with-classes; horror/emergence is low, and mission types repeat (grind of the same objective set) `[inference from reviews above]`.

### Barotrauma (Undertow Games)
Why it works
- Roles (captain, engineer, medic, security, mechanic) on a shared submarine; survival depends on communication under cascading failures (flooding, wiring, monsters). https://coopgames.com/game/95/barotrauma
- Emergent stories from unplanned disasters: crew deaths, sub destruction, and comedy of miscommunication. https://beforeyoubuy.games/602960/Barotrauma
- Deep systems (medical, wiring, pressure physics) give expert players mastery.
Weaknesses
- Steep learning curve, tutorials do not cover systems, confusing UI; single-player AI is weak. https://vaporlens.app/app/602960/barotrauma , https://cogconnected.com/review/barotrauma-review/

### Escape from Tarkov (extraction loop only)
Why it works
- Loss aversion: death forfeits everything carried ("gear fear"), so accumulated loot raises tension; extraction becomes the real objective. https://kotaku.com/a-shooter-where-you-risk-losing-your-gear-forever-1821961661 , https://twincitiesgeek.com/2018/11/escape-from-tarkov-is-stress-inducing-in-the-best-way-possible/
- Extraction points are contested endgame moments (campers, ambush): the final hurdle after looting. https://attackofthefanboy.com/guides/escape-from-tarkov-how-to-extract/
- Player decides risk budget (loadout wagered), so tension is self-authored. https://www.businessage.com/post/risk-vs-reward-in-tarkov-analyzing-the-impact-of-high-end-gear-and-wealth-on-extraction-success-rates
- Extraction shooters are the ancestor of "take loot, get out or lose it"; friendslop adds comedy in place of hardcore loss. `[inference]`
Weakness
- Punishing loss and cheaters/hostile lobbies create a high skill and time barrier; not accessible to casual groups. https://insider-gaming.com/love-or-hate-extraction-shooters-wild/

### Risk of Rain 2 (Hopoo)
Why it works
- Power fantasy through item stacking; the game embraces exploitation and power creep ("shredding a boss in five seconds"). https://www.pcgamer.com/risk-of-rain-2-review/
- Ebb-and-flow co-op: players scatter to find items/chests, regroup for teleporter boss. https://www.pcgamer.com/risk-of-rain-2-is-the-co-op-game-i-keep-coming-back-to/
- Difficulty clock forces pacing (time is the threat).
Weakness
- Item stacking snowballs onto one player, others fall behind and spectate; multiplayer feels hit-or-miss. https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/1640917832747380435/ , https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/1812044473325787501/

### Valheim (Iron Gate)
Why it works
- Unequal interests: builder, farmer/cook, scout, fighter each find their niche, group meets for bosses and dangerous landings; shared persistent world. https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/valheim-1-0-review-pixel-twelve , https://5gamers.com/en/valheim/guides/co-op
- Disaster-to-story generation (boat, boss, tombstones, "who forgot the portal").
- Base as emotional anchor: progress belongs to the shared world.
Weaknesses
- Grind; 1.0 exposed the cost of long Early Access (biome generation bugs). https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/valheim-1-0-review-pixel-twelve

### Dredge / Inscryption ("the game watches you", relevance only)
- Inscryption: the "game" is revealed as footage of a YouTuber's playthrough (Luke Carder), entities know they are in a game and compete for control; layered fourth-wall breaks. Effective because they break the frame the player trusted. https://www.geeksundergrace.com/gaming/review-inscryption/ , https://gamerant.com/inscryption-meta-narrative-card-games-stand-out-selling-point/ , https://www.thegamer.com/best-horror-games-that-go-meta/
- Lesson for TFG: meta-horror lands when it uses *the player's real state* (their saved data, their footage, their behaviour), and when the rules of the frame are kept consistent. `[inference]`
- Dredge: no source retrieved; skip citing.

### Peak (Landfall, 2025, $8, 15.4M copies) (recent comparison)
- Dev in 4 months after a game jam; one central mechanic (damageable stamina bar) plus wobbly elastic characters and physics humour; bbno$ in-game concert = live event. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_(video_game) , https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/from-game-jam-to-10-million-units-indie-hit-peak-success

## 2. Cross-cutting success factors (ranked)

1. **One strong verb, instantly readable in a 30-second clip.** Grab-with-physics (R.E.P.O.), film-your-friend (Content Warning), climb on stamina (Peak). https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025
2. **Diegetic proximity voice as the communication constraint.** Lethal Company: voice echo, cut-offs on death, silence when separated. https://www.gamedesignlibrary.com/post/proximity-chat-changes-the-game-how-lethal-company-s-game-design-innovated-multiplayer-horror-games
3. **Loss-aversion extraction loop: loot is only real if you get out.** Tarkov "gear fear"; Lethal Company loot lost with the dead carrier. https://kotaku.com/a-shooter-where-you-risk-losing-your-gear-forever-1821961661
4. **Cheap, low-friction entry: $8-10 impulse price, no tutorial gate, first-session payoff.** Peak $7.99, Lethal/R.E.P.O. $9.99; one purchase pulls in the group (social multiplier). https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025
5. **Physics/comedy as the failure state (failing is funny, not humiliating).** R.E.P.O. heavy objects, monsters obeying gravity; Peak elastic climbers. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/
6. **Simple monsters with rule-based counterplay that force a behaviour change.** Coil-Head (stare/turn), Huntsman (sound), DRG classes answering swarms. Counter-example: Content Warning "just avoid" monsters. https://steamcommunity.com/app/2881650/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated
7. **Asymmetric, role-diverse co-op so everyone matters.** Lethal ship operator; DRG classes; Barotrauma roles; Valheim builders vs scouts. Counter: Risk of Rain 2 item-stacking leaves others spectating. https://steamcommunity.com/app/632360/discussions/0/1640917832747380435/
8. **Short sessions + pacing contrast (calm, spike, escape).** DRG 10-40 min missions; Phasmophobia investigate-then-hunt. https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2020/05/deep-rock-galactic-review-an-otherworldly-co-op-experience/
9. **Built-in content-creator loop (watchability and clip-ability).** Lethal streamer boom; Content Warning's SpookTube; Peak in-game concert. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-can-we-learn-from-lethal-companys
10. **Post-launch cadence and mod/content variety to fight decay.** R.E.P.O. Monster Update + free weekend; Lethal decays to modded lobbies; friendslop D30 retention only ~3%, so updates are the retention lever. https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/you-can-now-twerk-enemies-to-death-in-repo-thanks-to-the-big-new-monster-update-and-its-free-to-play-this-weekend-to-celebrate/ , https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025

## 3. Why Lethal Company clones failed

Evidence base is thin: no clone post-mortems surfaced, so most points below combine sourced market data with `[inference]`.

- **Market math.** The median paid Steam game earns a few hundred dollars; a handful capture most revenue; "for every hit, hundreds of clones sell almost nothing". https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025
- **Copying the surface, not the mechanism.** Low-poly visuals, quota, scrap are easy to clone; the diegetic-voice-driven social tension and monster rule design are not. "Even experienced developers underestimated the game's complexity." https://www.avclub.com/not-every-co-op-game-is-friendslop , https://www.destructoid.com/friendslop-is-ruining-horror-games-and-im-tired-of-it/ (via search summaries)
- **No new verb.** R.E.P.O. (physics grab) and Content Warning (filming) are the clones that broke out, because each added one distinctive core action; "same but with minor tweaks" did not. https://gamerant.com/co-op-horror-game-like-lethal-company-hit-steam-pc-repo/ , https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/repo-dev-says-it-wasnt-actually-inspired-by-lethal-company-and-started-as-a-singleplayer-cleaning-game-it-was-nice-but-far-from-what-repo-is-now/
- **Cash-grab intent.** Critics argue many friendslop games purposely limit ambition to go viral, sell, and move on. https://www.gamegrin.com/articles/is-friendslop-really-that-bad/ (via search summary)
- **Retention cliff and no content plan.** D30 ~3% average; Lethal Company itself fell 240k -> ~5k CCU; without updates/mods a clone dies in weeks. https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025
- **Streamer-fit failure.** Games must produce readable, funny clips within minutes; lo-fi clones with slow or technically buggy loops (networking, physics jank) yield no shareable moments. `[inference]`
- **Networking/technical polish.** Physics and netcode are the hard parts (R.E.P.O.'s own weakness). A clone with desync loses the comedy. https://gamerant.com/co-op-horror-game-like-lethal-company-hit-steam-pc-repo/
- **Repetition:** identical interiors, few monsters, thin late game. Even the original suffers here. https://www.zleague.gg/theportal/lethal-company-gamers-speak-out-on-the-top-issues/
- **Saturation.** Dozens of near-identical titles competed for the same friend-group's one purchase. https://www.eneba.com/hub/games/games-like-lethal-company/ (context)

## 4. Ten ideas for a browser co-op horror game with an "AI that watches and live-streams you" villain

Constraints: Vite + three.js + Rapier + Trystero P2P; no install; short sessions; joining by link.

1. **The Stream Is the Villain's Health Bar (audience-driven threat).** The AI streamer has a fake live chat with viewer count; monsters spawn and behaviour shift in reaction to "chat votes" triggered by what the crew does (loud, panicking, sitting still = boring = punish). Content Warning views logic inverted: the *villain* wants views. https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/content-warning-a-free-co-op-horror-game-where-you-go-viral-on-spooktube-or-die-trying-is-rapidly-climbing-the-steam-charts/
2. **Boredom Meter / Director.** Use a menace-gauge-style AI Director (Alien: Isolation, L4D). The AI tracks "entertainment value" of the crew; when the crew plays safe, it escalates; when they are overwhelmed, it eases and stages a jump-scare "moment" for the stream. https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-perfect-organism-the-ai-of-alien-isolation
3. **Camera-in-world: visible surveillance.** Ceiling cameras/drones with a red "LIVE" light; you are only "seen" when in a lens cone. Killing/blinding the camera blocks the villain's *information* (and its viewer count) but also its aid to you (e.g. door unlocks). Stealth is a spatial puzzle.
3b. **Broadcast delay.** The stream is delayed 5 s, so the AI acts on stale info; players can "spoof" by leaving decoys (a physics prop or a voice-clip) that the AI then reacts to. Rewards deception between teammates.
4. **The AI quotes you.** Record short proximity-voice fragments (opt-in, local, never uploaded beyond peers) and replay/remix them from speakers or via monsters (Inscryption-style frame break, plus Lethal-style diegetic voice). Real-player-state horror: "the game uses what you actually said". https://www.geeksundergrace.com/gaming/review-inscryption/
5. **Highlight-reel end screen with an auto-generated clip.** At the end of each run the villain "edits" the top 3 moments (deaths, close calls, funniest physics) into a shareable 10 s replay (client-side re-render of stored transform snapshots). Clip-ability is factor #9; free marketing. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-can-we-learn-from-lethal-companys
6. **Sponsored challenges from the villain ("donations").** Random optional constraints with payout multipliers: "carry it with only 1 flashlight", "no talking for 30 s", "swap loot carriers". Creates DRG-style risk-budget choices and comedy. Counter to Content Warning's linear scaling. https://steamcommunity.com/app/2881650/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated
7. **Physics-comedy loot that the AI can hijack.** R.E.P.O.-style heavy/fragile loot in Rapier; the AI can spend "viewer donations" to knock props, flicker lights, or greased floors. Failure is funny. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/
8. **Role asymmetry through one player being "the Operator" who sees the villain's dashboard.** A Lethal ship-operator analogue: one player views the fake chat, the AI's "attention target" and its viewer count, but can only communicate by proximity radio. Everyone else is blind. Fixes "nothing to do while others explore". https://lethalcompanyreview.com/lethal-company-review-a-masterclass-in-chaotic-co-op-horror/
9. **Meta-persistence: the watcher remembers you across runs.** Store locally (per-player, device-bound) a profile of habits (always hides in lockers? always hugs the wall? mic loud?) and the AI opens with counters and taunts from prior sessions. Inscryption/meta-horror payoff, and gives retention without a huge content bill (retention is the genre's weak point: ~3% D30). https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025
10. **Loss-aversion extraction with a "cut the feed" exit.** Loot is banked only when the crew reaches the exit AND stays off-camera for N seconds ("cut the stream"); dead players' loot is left as a drop. Tarkov-style gear fear in a 15-min session, browser-friendly. https://kotaku.com/a-shooter-where-you-risk-losing-your-gear-forever-1821961661

Design guardrails to avoid clone failure (from section 3)
- Ship with one clear verb (say: "dodge the camera's gaze while carrying wobbly loot") and prove it in a 30-s clip before adding content.
- Treat instant join (URL), free/cheap entry and 10-15 min sessions as differentiators: the browser removes the "one friend didn't buy it" friction.
- Budget for post-launch rotation: modular monsters/"stream events" and daily seeded runs, since retention is the genre-wide problem.
- Keep the villain's rules legible (players must be able to say "it saw me because ..."), like Coil-Head/Huntsman.
