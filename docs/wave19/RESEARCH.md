Primary sources were fetched successfully from the public Steam store during this round. These
are developer/publisher descriptions, not a claim that the agent played those commercial games.
For each game, the public Steam reviews endpoint was also sampled with `filter=all`, English,
12 positive and 12 negative reviews requested independently. That deliberately balanced, small,
helpfulness-ranked sample is qualitative evidence; it cannot estimate prevalence, retention or
the overall audience's opinion. Not every English-filtered response was actually English.

## TFG's own identity

Read `CLAUDE.md`, the current cloud instructions in `AGENTS.md`, `docs/HANDOFF.md`, `docs/THEME.md`
and wave18's independent review before choosing work. The strongest native direction is a crew
salvaging a dead internet while the Algorithm watches and monetises it. Keep faceted PSX industrial
workwear, warm worn service colors, black terminal faces, limited status pixels and restrained
magenta authority accents. Readability should come from value separation and landmarks, rather
than broad luminous green, glossy inflated models or extra scene lights.

The ship/quota/extraction skeleton is still familiar. Originality should grow through consequences:
surveillance can reduce cargo value, physical camera power can close an optional route, archived
broadcasts can redirect sound-reactive creatures, and crew roles can split the risk of operating a
station from carrying the haul. The point is more meaningful choices in an existing expedition.

## This round's decisions

1. Improve ordinary-exposure worker contrast without changing geometry, rigs or light count.
2. Make the actual ship entrance identifiable and physically validate its approach across hulls.
3. Diagnose the recurring framebuffer warning at the offending draw before changing rendering.
4. Correct demonstrated counterplay and interaction-selection defects with native-module tests.
5. Add one optional Dead-Air Replay control to the authored broadcast district. A warning and finite
   native-noise diversion let a crew coordinate a haul. Existing camera power and real creature
   perception remain relevant; there is no new compulsory upkeep, currency or reward faucet.

The prior independent score is 7.7/10. A new score belongs to the independent reviewer after actual
interaction and visual evidence. More features, more tests and a target of eight do not themselves
prove enjoyment. Human crew sessions, headphone feedback, Internet latency and representative
hardware performance remain material evidence outside local software-WebGL fixtures.
