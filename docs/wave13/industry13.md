# Field brokers, commissioned production and robot surveys

The physical field broker replaces buying equipment directly from the ship. A staffed workshop exists on the Relay Dock, the company exchange plaza (20,12), and standard moon ship aprons (11,9). Expedition/custom maps without a safe standard apron are intentionally skipped. E opens the workshop; TOOLS opens the existing item shop with existing deal prices and stock locks. Equipment is delivered at the broker's feet, not magically inside the ship. Ship terminal remains usable for routing/catalogue; buy/cart/upgrade/van orders require physical proximity to a broker on the host. Old ship and HQ kiosk prompts are removed while this system is installed. Existing personal black-market/special shops retain their own NPC flows.

Workshop has three shared crew bays. Commission a batch of rebuilt power cells (12→23 Credits, one completed field shift), medicinal culture (20→35, one shift), or fictional Dreamdust contraband (30→52, two shifts). Finished batches are sold manually at a broker. These are fictional economy commodities, not instructions about real drugs. Contraband has a bounded customs-seizure chance and readable customs heat; legal output is reliable. There is no offline income, AFK timer, passive quota credit or infinite free product. Broker payout per completed shift is capped at max(55,min(100,quota×0.12)); production bays, batch custody and limited working capital constrain growth.

Dispatch one robot scout for 45 Credits. After two completed field shifts it returns 52–68 salvage Credits and the surveyed route/weather report. Choose an unlocked-tier ordinary destination in the panel; another robot cannot be dispatched until the prior report is claimed. Report collect is exactly once. This is a mission-level passive scout abstraction, not a separately simulated robot navigating every moon in real time.

Progress records the actual takeoff phase in run.industry13.departure, then settles on the orbit phase after the day increments. This supports both deferred completion and fault-repaired launches which call captured methods and bypass outer wrappers. The serialized departure also survives host replacement during takeoff; duplicate orbit events cannot mature another batch. HQ/home/dock visits do not advance production. State travels through run.industry13, ordinary welcome/save/host-migration snapshots; credits remain the existing crew wallet. Requests validate living requester and nearby trader. Network `i13req` is request-only, `i13reply` is host-only.

Validation: industry13.test.mjs covers credits/custody, repeat sell/report/takeoff rejection, bay and payout caps, malformed catalogue IDs, two-shift scouting, and actual module deferred-takeoff regression. Dedicated browser QA is recorded separately in PLAYTEST.md. Hands-on production equipment, item ingredients, visible robot travel and production logistics are future depth; this implementation is commissioned production with genuine campaign time/capital decisions.
