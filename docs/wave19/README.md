## Implemented scope

- **Readable industrial workers:** neutral jacket/trouser/pocket values and functional gloves/helmet
  details separate at ordinary exposure. Black CRT faces, PSX facets, rigs, bounds and draw counts remain.
- **Reliable ship return:** four native hulls have climbable entrance treads and painted threshold
  edges. Company/field entry guidance is brief, pauses during danger and does not recreate forever.
- **Fair machine counterplay:** Line Printer and Checksum stop finishing attacks against incapacitated
  players, then resume ordinary threats after revival. Scanner instructions match the neutral art.
- **Usable prompts:** hidden/empty dynamic interaction labels cannot swallow the next usable station.
  Physical item/door priority and line-of-sight remain.
- **Dead-Air Replay:** one physical recording per landing in Relay Ward. After an eight-second warning,
  three native noise pulses across a twelve-second broadcast can draw sound-reactive creatures away
  from a hauling route. Cutting camera power cancels it; real perception and obstruction remain.
- **Bodycam framebuffer fix:** its own monitor sampled the same texture being written by its feed.
  The feed temporarily hides that screen and restores prior visibility/state in `finally`, preserving
  the live feature. Direct browser GL producer evidence and an actual built-in-mod fixture identified
  the path outside the earlier `src/`-only audit.
