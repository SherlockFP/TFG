# Wave41 native maintenance robots

New installer `installEndless41Robots(game)` exposes `tick(dt)`, `buy(id,from)`, `catalog(from=game.selfId)` and `dispose()`. Root invokes the tick once per normal module update; this worker adds no second simulation clock or shared-file modifications. Robots are available only when both native run/config select Endless and the version 41 ledger exists. The root's `e41req` handler owns token/revision validation; the robot purchase helper repeats host/current-mode/known-connected-living-player/preparation/floor/range/static-door LOS checks at `stationPoint()`, and finite-wallet/rank/cap checks.

The catalog contains hauler 80 credits, guardian 100 credits, hauler upgrades 60/100 credits and guardian upgrades 80/130 credits. Each owner can have one living robot of each role, ranks 1–3, with at most eight living robots total. Upgrade entries expose `available:false` until that owner's robot exists. Root's preparation UI should honor that prerequisite. Native `run.credits`, robot ownership/rank, nextRobot and ledger revision change before synchronous state publication; re-entry cannot duplicate a purchased robot. No separate wallet exists.

Custody uses the existing native trolley convention: `it held` with holder `c:e41robot:<id>`, followed by the existing native `it drop` on release. ItemManager remains the owner of the one item ID/type/value/body; the robot attaches that exact item object's visual to its bounded carry anchor on every host/peer tick. No extra item, cargo inventory, creature holder view or shared ItemManager/Game change is introduced. Native manual pickup/grab cannot steal held robot cargo. A hauler selects one unheld, unowned, sellable, unsoulbound small exterior scrap within 22 m of its living owner. Native bounds limit cargo to 0.6 m horizontally and 0.4 m vertically; large salvage, bodies, deployed ladders and apparatus are excluded.

Haulers acquire only after their actual kinematic body reaches physical pickup range and LOS. A bounded quarter-metre A* route uses native static/door floor normals, real capsule occupancy, slope/step limits and edge LOS. The native Rapier controller owns contacts, autostep, snap and movement; every computed movement, including vertical-only stair depenetration, is applied. No body/cargo teleport substitutes for a blocked route. Planning is capped at 2048 nodes and 140 m, candidate selection eight items, and retries throttled. Cargo returns along the actual open doorway and stairs to a physically certified native airlock position insideShip, then drops from its actual robot-body/carry pose. The ordinary ship extraction/cashout remains root/native ownership.

Guardians follow their connected living owner, attack only `game.endless41.ownsCreature(c)` live threats, require actual range ≤8 m and static/door LOS, and invoke the native CreatureManager.damage with owner credit and a bounded cooldown. Rank upgrades improve damage/cooldown; root temporary guardian firmware scales damage and haul firmware scales transport speed. Robots do not create profile XP or a second kill ledger. Native wave rules remain root/wave ownership.

During the manual departure warning, robots turn back to the airlock. Actual takeoff releases cargo exactly once at the robot's current physical position; a robot still outside becomes lost rather than transporting outside goods into orbit. Unload/session end drops owned custody before native cleanup and removes runtime bodies, controllers and visuals. Surface route streaming does not move exterior robots. Replicated pure JSON robot records survive welcome/state and host promotion; no extra purchase debit or cargo spawn occurs during reconstruction.

Art is original procedural geometry: a matte four-wheel basket hauler and a braced CRT-faced guardian with three muted Lambert batches each. Dirty ivory, charcoal, worn steel and restrained ochre distinguish roles. There are no new lights, emissive surfaces or externally sourced assets.
