# Wave 8 - resto: TFG ALIEN DINER (Roblox-style restaurant tycoon on the homeworld)

Owner: "let us open a restaurant on our own planet, tycoon-style, grow it, alien customers, with a purpose and logic".
Module `src/game/resto.js` (+ `resto_core.js` pure rules, `resto_i18n.js`, `src/world/resto_view.js` models, `src/ui/panels/resto.js` panel, `src/minigames/resto_pan.js` station minigame), slot `resto`. Test: `node tools/harness/resto.test.mjs` (14 checks). Net (prefix `rs`): `rsreq` client->host `{op}`, `rsx` host->all (live sim snapshot, 4 Hz), `rsmsg` host->client (`err/toast/banner/built/dep/till/pay/cooked/cookok/angry/pest/swat/land`). `rsx`/`rsmsg` are in `HOST_ONLY`.

## The loop
1. **Build (tycoon pads).** East of the base, outside the build square and off the approach lanes / decor footprints: plot x 48.4..56.6, z 28..44.8, shuttle pad north of the door at (52.5, 23.4). An empty plot shows corner posts + a sign ("Alien Diner [E]" opens the panel). Glowing pads on the floor show what can be bought next (green = affordable, amber = too expensive, red = needs stars). Stand on a pad 0.7 s: the host validates distance / chain / stars / credits, the piece pops in (overshoot scale, dust, sparks, `ui_buy`). 20 pieces: Foundation (floor, walls, pass counter), Stove x3, Fridge, Register, Tables 1-6, Critic's booth, Neon sign, Decor I/II/III, Chef / Waiter / Cashier robots. Floors / rugs / pads use `flatLayer` (no PSX z-fight).
2. **Guests.** A shuttle lands (4 s descent), 1-4 aliens walk in, queue, take a seat, sit, order. Patience bars (queue / order / food phases; a dirty diner drains 35 % faster); unhappy guests leave and cost reputation. Species (taste rules): Gorm Grazer (anything, patient), Blorp (raw / moon produce only), Vrek Emberkin (spicy only, short temper, pays x1.3), Klink Scrapper (pays in rare scrap items), Mimi Sweettooth (sweet only, tips Clout), Reviewer-9 (Algorithm food critic, only sits in the booth, wants a perfect dish), Health Inspector.
3. **Logic (why they come).** A species only flies in when the UNLOCKED menu has a dish it accepts AND the fridge can cook something right now (no guest flies here to starve). Frequency = 62 s - 6 s/star - 8 s (sign) - tables, +-15 %. No shuttle when closed, out of stock, or nobody is on the homeworld. Leaving the homeworld clears the guests.
4. **Cook.** Guests order from dishes built from ingredient CATEGORIES (leaf / mushroom / root / berry / moss / fruit / meat / fish + new moon, spice, rare): existing farming / foraging / fishing / creature-meat items all count (farmed Ashroot and Frostleaf even count as a weak spice). Fridge: E stocks every ingredient you hold. Stove: E on the oldest open ticket -> `rs_pan` minigame (3-6 s heat needle, stop in the gold; same zones as the survival cooking meter). The host reserves the ingredients at start, clamps the claimed quality by its own clock (`clampQuality`), refunds on cancel, and spawns a REAL plate item (`rs_d_<dish>`, quality = item tier) on the pass counter. Carry it to the table (E "Serve"), guests eat 7 s, leave a dirty seat and walk to the register; E "Take payment" pays 100 %, auto-pay after 9 s pays 85 %. Bus tables with E. 10 dishes, unlocked by stars (3 / 5 / 8 / 9 / 10 dishes at 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 stars).
5. **Purpose.** Good dishes need moon-only ingredients: Moonpetal (outdoors, 1-3 per landing at outdoor scrap spots), Ember Pepper + Glow Spore Cap (facility scrap spots, 1-2 each), Void Truffle (deep facility spot, 16 % + 2 %/quota, max 36 %), seeded by the host on `moonPopulated` with a seeded RNG. Income is CAPPED: active takings per game day (100 + 70/star, e.g. 240 at 2 stars, 450 at 5; past it guests pay 25 %) and passive income per game day (6 + 9/star, i.e. 51 at 5 stars) into a till that holds 3 days (collect at the register). The homeworld has no clock, so game days pass only on real runs: fly a run to reset the cap AND to restock. Quota runs stay the main money (a mid-game active day is ~480 credits, `ACTIVE_DAY_VALUE`).
6. **Stars 1-5** = min(reputation stars, decor cap). Reputation 0 / 25 / 80 / 180 / 350; happy serve +1 (+2 perfect), burnt -1, angry leave -3. Decor I / II / III raise the cap 2 -> 3 / 4 / 5. Stars gate pieces (tables 4-5 at 2, robots + booth + table 6 + Decor II at 3, stove 3 + Decor III at 4) and the menu.
7. **Automation (solo works).** Chef robot cooks open orders itself (cooked quality, 20 % perfect) while stock lasts, on every built stove; Waiter robot takes orders, carries finished plates from the pass and clears tables; Cashier robot pays every guest at full price.
8. **Food Festival contract.** From 3 stars the Algorithm offers one every 7 game days (accept in the panel): serve 10-16 cooked-or-better dishes within 7 game days -> 400 + 220/star credits (5 stars: 1500) and 10 Clout each + a viewer spike.
9. **Critic.** With the booth, 3+ stars, every ~4 min: Reviewer-9 orders the priciest dish; perfect = rave (+10 rep, +6 Clout each, viewers via `VIEW_GAIN.review` +30 %), cooked = +2 rep, worse = pan (-8 rep).
10. **Risk (light).** Each finished meal leaves a dirty seat (`clean = 100 - 12 per dirty table - 15 per pest`). 2+ dirty tables for 30 s spawn pests (max 3): low-poly bugs that eat one fridge item per 18 s, swat with E. Away for a game day with dirt / pests: the fridge loses 30 %. Health Inspector every 6 game days: clean >= 70 pass (+80 + 40/star credits, +10 rep), 40-70 warning (-2 rep), < 40 fined (60 + 30/star credits, -8 rep). Not a creature raid (that would need siege AI): abstract on purpose.
11. **Co-op.** Roles: cook (stove), waiter (order / serve / bus), cashier (register / till), forager (ingredients from moons), exterminator. The host owns sim / pantry / credits / spawns; every peer renders the same snapshot; requests are distance-validated and rate-limited.
12. **UI.** No new HUD widget: interactables + floor pads + one management panel (STATUS / BUILD / MENU / GUESTS) on `ui.panel` + `tfg-card / tfg-bar / tfg-tag`. EN + TR + RU (`resto_i18n.js`, only fills keys other modules did not translate).

## State
`profile.resto` (host, `sanitize` in `resto_core`, survives runs) = `run.rs` (synced via `broadcastRun(['rs'])`, late joiners get it in `welcome`). Fields: `b` built ids, `rep`, `pantry {type:n}`, `till`, `open`, `mk` day mark, `gross {day,n}`, `ct` contract, `ctDone/ctAt/inspAt`, `st` stats. The live sim (customers / pests / shuttle / dirty seats) is host memory only, never saved.

## Knobs
`resto_core.js`: `PIECES` (cost / req / star / at / pad), `DISHES` (need / price / dur / star), `SPECIES` (pat / speed / mul / pay / likes), `STAR_REP`, `DECOR_CAP`, `ECON` (passive, day cap, over-cap 25 %, rep deltas, contract, inspector), `EAT_SEC / PAY_AUTO / COOK_TIMEOUT`, `arrivalEvery`, `MAX_PANTRY(_TYPE)`. Ingredient drop odds: `seedIngredients()` in `resto.js`.
