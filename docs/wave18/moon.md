# Wave 18 hero moon — Relay Ward

56K-Dialup (`hamsi`) keeps its existing name, hills biome, factory interior, loot, fare and progression. Its seeded ship-to-entrance path now runs through an authored broadcast street that is present on the first day. Other moons do not build or reserve this component.

Four distinct asymmetric facades share the existing PSX materials: a brick reel house with paired reels, a tall transmitter with antenna crossbars and rooftop equipment, a low switchboard frontage with four panel banks, and a dead studio with a broken waveform fascia, awning and physical cover. Foundation bottoms follow each footprint's sampled terrain while roofs and trim follow its local highest ground. Window glows remain deliberately muted. Buildings and useful low cover have complete solid Rapier colliders; all static scenery merges into eight material batches. The moving shutter adds two meshes, with no new Three lights or light-pool emitters.

The real seeded main path, ship spawn, main entrance and fire entrances remain clear. A sheltered service passage sits beside the street and joins it at both ends. It is optional; the normal main return does not depend on power. A raised shutter and physical gantry communicate the open state. Cutting network power lowers the visible shutter and creates a real collider across this side passage. Restoring power removes its collider and raises the shutter. Crew occupancy validation belongs to the host gameplay module; world API `canClose` is available for an additional oriented capsule-volume check. A person on either side of a closed gate can still leave through that side's street connection.

Planning happens before outdoor scatter. Only the actual facade foundations, service passage and its street connectors reserve space, so rocks, trees, outposts and landmarks cannot occupy those footprints. Compact seeded approaches use a wider side setback and a shorter optional alley. If that real footprint is unsafe, planning deterministically searches another entry/exit pair, the opposite side, or an outward setback; ship/fire/pond exclusion distances are never reduced. The full proposed passage, its street connectors and console approach are checked against the actual building and low-cover footprints. Unsafe facades independently try a nearby outward setback, then the opposite side; a fire exit or pond near one building does not discard unrelated buildings. The whole ward gracefully skips only if it cannot establish three safe facades and the real passage footprint. Seed 17's compact approach is explicitly covered by regression checks.

## World/gameplay contract

`outdoor.broadcast18` exposes immutable token-independent `plan`, owned `group`, `setPowered(bool)`, `canClose(pos, radius=.45, height=1.8)`, `gateCollider` getter, `powered` getter and idempotent `dispose()`.

`plan.console` is a THREE.Vector3 on the street-facing side outside the passage wall. Its stand collider ends below the screen anchor so normal STATIC/DOOR interaction rays can reach it. `plan.shortcutDoor` provides `{ pos, size: [localXWidth, height, localZWidth], rotY, baseY }`. Gameplay owns landing token, host state, outage duration and safe closure decisions; this component owns no currency, rewards or timer.

QA waypoints: `plan.waypoints.approach`, `.street`, `.relay`, `.entrance`, `.console`; `mainReturn` holds the seeded path points; `serviceRoute` holds the street-entry-alley-gate-exit-street route; `serviceEntry`, `serviceExit` and `basepoints` expose actual ground positions. Use eye-height street viewpoints and face the transmitter/reel-house bases for silhouette review. Gate position is its collider centre, not a walking-foot position.

Terrain integration is limited to an import, seeded preplan, reservation hook, component build/exposure and disposal call. Disposal removes only owned geometry/material clones and colliders; cached shared facility materials are not disposed.

## Validation

`node tools/harness/broadcast18_world.test.mjs` passes on eleven complete actual outdoor maps (the five baseline seeds 17, 1235, 1234, 777, 42 plus six formerly rejected/problem seeds), each with four facades. Tests use real Rapier and check that main return rays do not hit any new ward geometry, a closed shortcut stops an actual player capsule, an open shortcut lets that capsule cross, the entire open service route avoids authored solids, console line of sight is clear, occupancy math is oriented correctly, and mesh count stays at ten or fewer with zero lights. A full Forum build confirms other-moon exclusion. A separate deterministic 100-seed plan audit improved from 81 available wards before the fallback to 100/100 afterward (98 four-facade plans, two three-facade plans). It uses the real planMoon/Terrain constructors without expensive full map builds. Four sampled seeds need a 32–36m optional alley setback; the main street/return keeps its ordinary route. This sample is evidence of improved identity consistency, not a guarantee for all 32-bit seeds. This is physics/world validation; browser visual assessment belongs to the scheduled QA run.

This pass establishes one recognizable hero street and a real optional power-dependent passage. Authored interior rooms, additional moons and economy changes are intentionally outside this lane's scope.
