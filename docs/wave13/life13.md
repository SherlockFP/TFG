# Life 13 — alien travelers and rival survey crews

Installation: import `installLife13` from `./life13.js`, then `this.useModule('life13', installLife13)` after fleet13. New files only: module, pure core, local EN/TR/RU text registry and procedural model module.

Five alien citizens inhabit both the dock and exchange. Three deliberately different silhouettes (winged prism glider, three-legged barrel observer, horned shell walker) avoid hazmat recolors. They travel short curated loops, wait briefly at corners, breathe and move their limbs, and face nearby visitors. Conversations explain the dock, contracts, company and survival loop. NPCs are neutral geometry outside the creature registry: no attacks, kills, drops or combat economy.

On ordinary moons, up to two survey agents stay near the ship approach. The first offers a short voluntary beacon survey; the second offers a clear alternative, barter of the cheapest eligible carried scrap. NPC interactions replace the generic talk prompt when there is an available action, making both choices physically discoverable. A mint geometric beacon is placed only on clear terrain. Accept -> walk to beacon -> mark -> report to first agent pays 18 team Credits. At least 8 seconds before marking and 15 before payout. Barter removes one lowest-value held scrap for floor(value * .3), minimum 1 and maximum 12 Credits; quota sold does not increase. Tools, weapons, equipment, bag items, soulbound items and bodies are protected. Survey and barter share one reward slot per crew per seed/day: re-landing, rebuilding, host migration and visiting another moon cannot duplicate payment.

State is host authoritative in run.life13; requests verify location token, living/not-downed/outside-ship player, actor/beacon proximity, line of sight and a 650ms rate limit. No client coordinates or amounts are accepted. The reward slot is reserved before item/network callbacks. Movement follows deterministic routes from a host clock sent every two seconds; no actor snapshots, lights, physics bodies or navigation searches are added. New route checks reject walls, body-margin clipping, blocked terrain and steep floor differences. A cheap forward check runs twice per second per actor.

Validation: one focused Node review using actual company and hub builders plus captured collider ray stubs confirms all five loops remain available in both cities, finite animation positions, model disposal, survey time gates and shared repeat-payment cap, cheapest-scrap host selection, quota preservation and reward-lock persistence across a rebuild. Existing company route assertions also pass. Browser and real multiplayer remain with QA. Some rugged/obstructed moons intentionally omit unsafe agents rather than place them inside scenery. Citizens do not physically block players. This provides ambient city activity and a small optional rival-crew encounter; full faction schedules, voiced dialogue and combat NPC factions are future work.
