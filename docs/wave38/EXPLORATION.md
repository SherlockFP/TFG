# Wave38 exploration, seeded routes and salvage

The owner requests discovery rewards, less filler loot, random industrial/maze/Backrooms content and removal of roaming exploration NPCs. The existing streamed depth facility and certified native lift remain the route owner. No second wallet, speculative portal, copied game asset or second simulation clock is introduced.

## Implemented behavior

- Fresh landing admission `exploration38:1` selects route version38. Depth1 immediately draws from the existing eleven industrial/archive maze palettes using the shared seeded RNG. Depth2 onward can draw a liminal theme with22% seeded chance; depth3 still guarantees the Backrooms reception, preserving a discoverable near-term destination. Old route26/35 saves retain their recorded seed/theme/layout. Every route still passes real native safe-cabin preflight; no fitting cabin means cancelled travel rather than a teleport bypass.
- The native descent room tracker also owns a bounded survey ledger. Each physically grounded, living crew visit to an unpaid room pays2 crew credits, increasing slowly with depth to a maximum5. Completing five available rooms pays20 credits plus a bounded depth bonus. At most15 room payments per floor. Only the current floor and surface ledgers remain in the save; abandoned depths cannot be revisited through the existing next-depth lifecycle. Surface return and save/reload cannot repeat payments. Existing visited rooms migrate as already accounted, without a retrospective payout.
- A host update, not a client money request, performs payment into `run.credits` and broadcasts/saves the native run state. Native current-facility containment, floor ray/normal, same-floor feet and eye-to-feet LOS are required for monetary rewards. The existing room visitation/lift admission contract remains separate; old fixture center placements do not fabricate paid discoveries.
- Active survey version38 reduces the lift requirement from15 rooms to five, or all available rooms on a smaller map. A bounded surface arrival cue explains credits, bonus, lift and Backrooms at depth3. The existing objective/full-status channel shows survey progress, crew earnings, native lift distance and upcoming destination; the physical call interaction also shows the five-room target. This follows the one-goal HUD contract rather than adding a new permanent overlay.
- Surface and deep-floor loose scrap counts are reduced with `lootCount38`, capped18, then rise with tier/depth. The existing theme tables remain authoritative, but expensive ordinary rolls receive much lower shallow-floor weights; depth, tier and farther rooms improve those weights. Native reactor, keys, first-haul Indexed Glass and guaranteed deep-room prize are preserved. No new reactor fallback was invented in this module.
- Actual roaming moon surveyors were identified in `life13.js`. Moon actor/beacon creation is retired. Dock/company citizens and finite courier dispatch/delivery remain intact.
