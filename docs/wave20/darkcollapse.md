# Darkness and optional ceiling collapse

Thread Archive and Buffer Foundry mark optional side rooms in `fac.lab.optionalRooms`. At most two eligible rooms per landing can lose one redundant doorway when a living crew remains in darkness for 45 seconds, followed by a full six-second cracking warning. A charged native flashlight in the room or a nearby powered native room emitter arrests pressure and makes it recede. Leaving also cancels the active warning and slowly reduces pressure. No feature-access gate, new currency, reward or damage is introduced.

The collapse is a partial ceiling failure at an optional passage, not the destruction of the whole room. Existing door physics supplies the permanent blocker; two bounded PSX meshes show the failing brace and fallen slabs, with no added lights. The player can use the room's other exit. The first warning uses native `door_creak`, a sagging amber brace and localized EN/TR/RU guidance. It does not create an instant death or move the crew.

## Safety and authority

Candidates are ordinary unlocked doors adjoining explicitly optional rooms. Entrance, generator, core, vault, arena, maze and required locked gates are excluded. Before selecting or committing a collapse, a flood of the actual native subcell NavGrid with the proposed edge removed must connect both doorway endpoints back to the main entrance. Every currently blocked edge, including prior collapses, remains excluded during that check. Crew and all world cargo/body footprints must clear the entire native door collider plus a margin before final commitment. If either safety condition fails, the warned passage remains open.

`installDarkCollapse20(game)` belongs after facility/expedition installers. It owns new files only, consumes existing theme metadata, and exposes `prepare()`, `state()`, `candidates()` and `dispose()`. Host `run.darkcollapse20={token,rev,rooms}` preserves finite candidate states, pressure, warning and permanent collapses. Pressure snapshots publish at most once a second between transitions; migration can therefore inherit at most one second less pressure than the departing host. Stage changes publish immediately. Host closure uses `Game.onDoor` and native `Game.updateDoors`, preserving real collider/NavGrid synchronization. Existing door/unlock/vault request paths reject collapsed IDs. Replicas apply the shared native lock, visuals and bounded audio without simulating their own pressure.

Light checks use native `aiPlayers().flash` and actual facility emitter intensity, range, room position and global `powerOn`. Existing MAPS2 room switches are local cosmetic changes; these new optional rooms do not rely on them as a shared safety control. Light pooling can limit rendered light count, but authoritative protection follows actual emitter sources rather than a peer's transient light allocation.

## Tests

`node tools/harness/darkcollapse20.test.mjs` builds four actual new-theme facilities (two themes, seeds 17 and 42) with real Rapier physics. It checks the long silent threshold, flashlight recovery, six-second warning, world-body deferral, permanent native collision and blocked navigation, return paths from both sides, old request bypass prevention, shared replica locks and migration of already locked candidates. In Thread Archive seed 17, an actual native player capsule walks the alternate return after other ordinary unlocked doors are opened normally; the collapsed edge remains blocked. A stalled-frame negative check prevents instant collapse after a long frame. Browser interaction, cue readability and human alternate-route walking remain QA responsibilities.
