# Wave38 company bell and chase margins

The company bell retains the real native cargo sale: the same counter collection, 2.6 second sale, cargo removal and crew credits/quota sold values. Bell authority now requires native eye range, static/door LOS, correct grounded floor and a living connected player. The discipline clock uses game time rather than wall time; older wall-clock locks are reset once when migrated.

Four empty rings retain the irritation warning. Six rapid empty rings warn that the intake claw is waking and start a 1.1 second windup. The original matte steel/bone claw can deal one bounded 38 damage hit only to that ringer, and only if that player remains physically at the bell. Stepping away during the warning avoids the hit. Other crew are not attacked because of another player's bell spam. Real deliveries reset irritation. Claw geometry/materials are owned by the company module and removed before map disposal; no new lights are added.

Two observed chase outliers were changed without increasing spawn budgets: regular Troll/hound run speed 11 → 6.4 m/s and Web Crawler 11 → 7 m/s. The hound's maximum regular late-sector/threat multiplier gives 8.0 m/s, below the unloaded player's 8.2 m/s sprint. Crawler retains its straight-line rush and poor turning. Other creature speeds, warning rules, spawn power, elite identities and normal horror models remain unchanged; this is not a claim that every monster has been remodeled or retuned.

Evidence: companybell38 first reproduced the absent scheduled attack, then verified real native company geometry, actual Session request self delivery, original hostSell economy/cargo callbacks, warned claw damage, retreat counterplay, dead/wall/floor/distance rejection and once-only presentation disposal. chase38 first reproduced regular hound outrunning player sprint at maximum normal scaling, then verified its new margin and preserved creature budget/other slower creature identity. Browser playability and aesthetic judgment remain root-owned.
