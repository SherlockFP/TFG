# Wave 14 — visible port identity and navigation

Before: Relay Dock was a large dark rectangular office with a flat selection desk; its sign faced away from the natural spawn approach. The exchange inherited a large industrial hall and selling hatch. Visitors had several physical destinations but weak ground-level directions.

After: The dock office has a forked relay crest, octagonal transfer aperture, lifted corner silhouettes, diagonal trusses and a mint selection fascia. A suspended transfer spine connects the existing vessel cranes. Company exchange has asymmetric archive fins and a matching signal coronet; a content-processing aperture frames the existing selling hatch, with a large CONTENT IN / SIGNAL OUT ticker above the route. Plum marks the casino entrance, amber the service/workshop district, mint the fleet/sale routes, ivory the boarding kiosk. Direction arrows and bordered stopping pads guide natural walking while keeping floor detail sparse.

All signs share one generated 1024x512 atlas per map, one sign mesh/draw call, with EN/TR/RU registration and live language repaint. New office signs face the actual spawn approach. Sign and floor paint opt out of vertex snapping; floor paint uses polygon offset, preventing coplanar PSX shimmer. No downloaded textures or THREE lights were added. New architecture and floor markings use GeoBuilder batches; each material is owned and released on map disposal. Existing cached metal texture remains shared.

Constraints: spawn, fleet broker, boarding console, field vendor, casino footprint/entrance, sale counter/drop zone/bell, contract board and all existing colliders/emitter registrations remain unchanged. Legacy company sign props retain collision/emitter registration with visuals hidden, so replacing signage does not alter collision paths or the light budget. New structure stays on existing wall/gantry footprints or overhead; painted routes add no collision. No new ramps or raised player routes were introduced: this pass improves discovery without changing traversal semantics.
