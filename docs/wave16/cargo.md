# Wave 16: bag salvage and conditional loaners

Wave 15's real copper pickup was valid: a full hotbar sends suitable salvage to the bag, while the selected torch remains selected. G consequently dropped the torch. Bag-held loot still counts aboard at extraction; changing that policy or automatically replacing selected tools would introduce a different problem.

`inventory_feedback16.js` runs after local host-held confirmation and inventory reconciliation. Positive-value, non-personal salvage pickups (excluding bodies and zero-value gifts) briefly state bag storage plus the actual inventory/drop bindings, or a non-selected hotbar slot plus its actual select binding. The existing loot feed labels every item's storage, using Carrying until a hotbar slot is known. Moving known bag salvage into the hotbar prompts its numbered selection. Duplicate confirmations are quiet (bounded 32-item cache). Dropping a tool aboard/company when salvage remains bagged keeps the native drop and gives a reminder, at most once per six seconds. No panel, auto-equip, net message, custody, price or quota change.

Audited native grants: onboarding locker equipment is a one-time tutorial reward; RPG role kits are intentional daily grants; survival starter food and profile loadouts already have their own guards. These remain unchanged. The proven redundancy was `loaner.js`: every fresh crew landing received another temporary light even with a usable purchased flashlight. The host now counts charged held flashlights/pro flashlights (including bag storage), and available charged ship-floor lights, and creates only the crew shortfall. Empty batteries do not count. Only an actual successful loan spawn emits the existing localized system announcement. Cleanup removes only tracked temporary loans, preserving bought/tutorial lights.

Validation: `node tools/harness/inventory_feedback16.test.mjs` exercises actual feedback/loaner modules, rebound keys, bag→hotbar confirmation without selection changes, original G behavior, replay/throttle/lifecycle, crew light shortfall, exhausted batteries, shared floor light and temporary cleanup. Negative cases cover remote pickups, bound gear, bodies and an actually bagged zero-value gift. Existing inventory, onboarding and first-run checks pass.

Shared browser QA subsequently confirmed actual E pickup into the native bag with visible Stashed in bag feedback, real I/right-click recovery, selected-item G and exact-ID bell settlement (30 at 34% → 10 credits). A recheck found the expanded bound-key pickup instruction in actual pendingToasts and the G reminder in toastQ. They are generated correctly but can wait behind startup/cinematic messages; immediate explanatory toast visibility is not claimed. See PLAYTEST.md for fixture boundaries and queue evidence.
