# Wave 17: production calibration

The three existing production bays retain their commissions, field-shift eligibility, finished parcels and single industry ledger. Eligible physical batches now offer **Calibrate batch [E]** instead of holding E for a timer.

- Rebuilt power cells: a pressure gauge cycles every 2.8 simulation seconds. Tap Space, Enter, the VALVE button or gamepad X when the marker is in the green band (.42–.78 of the cycle). Three successful pulses, at most one per cycle, prepare the parcel. The deliberately broad band permits ordinary latency; the host validates its own current clock.
- Culture/contraband: connect three numbered signal terminals in the displayed order. Click a terminal, use 1/2/3, arrows then Enter, or gamepad D-pad then X. Every confirmed connection visibly advances the sequence; waiting for host acknowledgment cannot itself advance it. Numbers/checkmarks avoid relying on color recognition.

Escape, the top-right ×, or gamepad B cancels. Missing/wrong actions, walking away, death, missing heartbeat, takeoff or timeout end only the attempt. The commissioned batch remains pending at its original cost; E retries with a fresh token. The world lever moves during local work, and existing green lamp/parcel becomes ready only after verified completion. Collection/sale remain separate physical interactions; no extra wallet, payout, quota credit or new robot system.

Browser QA found an end-of-shift case card could cover the pressure gauge while calibration accepted inputs underneath. A scoped theme rule now places only the workshop minigame layer at 41, above the case card's 40 and below dialogs' 45. Other minigames, cinematic queues and challenge timing are unchanged. The final UI fixture checks actual case-card/common-minigame coexistence separately from the native gameplay proof.

Personal attempts disappear on unload/migration; paid ledger jobs remain.
