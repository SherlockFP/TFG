# Start-open physical ship stations

The requested day-one access does not require gifting purchased shipyard rooms. All four fleet vessels share the native ship core: arcade/crafting anchors and cabinet are built by `buildShip`; `installIncubator` unconditionally attaches the two-slot pet incubator to the ship group; the food module independently places a real mess table in `TABLE_SPOTS`. No quota, level, room/module, upper-deck or shipyard purchase gate exists in those physical installers. The effective old pet gate is `game.onboard.deny('pets')` in `pets.open`, plus `hubgate`'s locked-position interaction replacement; arcade/tree have corresponding central gate handling. Root owns the user-authorized central start-open policy. Company owns the food-table lifecycle improvement.

Upper Deck tier zero is a purchased hull expansion (Mk I 450 credits, higher cabin/rooms paid), not a lock on accessing the existing systems. Shipyard module/room selection has no progression/level access gate; resources and effects remain paid. This lane deliberately leaves every owned module, tier, part balance, route effect and installed deck untouched. No fake feature flag or free maxed room is added.

`tools/harness/starter_stations18.test.mjs` runs real `buildShip`, `installShipyard`, `installIncubator`, and `installFood` with native Rapier across Courier/Hauler/Recovery/Survey, host and mirrored peer roles, level 1/day 1/quota 0, orbit/dock and field phases. It checks actual incubator geometry and native E callback, arcade cabinet/prompt line of sight, actual food-table placement/E, and native capsule walking to the incubator with the real food table colliders present. Empty E access opens the nest callback without creating an egg/pet or spending/granting money. Additive fleet sanitation preserves existing Mk III modules/deck and parts. The test passes.

The station test establishes the physical/callback contract; it does not replace root's central gate tests or claim the full DOM skill/pet/arcade panels were browser-tested. No shipyard/default-module production change was necessary for this audit. In particular, access to an incubator does not supply a free egg, bypass native item consumption, skip incubation days, or max pets.
