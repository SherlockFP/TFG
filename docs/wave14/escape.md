# Escape14: optional pursuit, physical shelter

Install once with `this.useModule('escape14', installEscape14)` from `./escape14.js`. Root owns game.js integration. No new mandatory minigame, default spawn weight or new progression currency.

From quota 2 onward eligible facility side rooms receive up to three physical screening alcoves: solid side/back/roof panels, mint entrance marker. Rooms must be at least 3×3 cells; entrance, vault, generator, arena and core/hub rooms are excluded. Real nav walkability and a floor ray reject unsuitable placements. Construction is deferred until two moon updates after mapLoaded, so Game.update has stepped Rapier and its spatial queries can see the newly created floor colliders; up to six bounded build attempts handle delayed readiness. Retries stop immediately if a shelter becomes occupied. Probing in mapLoaded previously produced zero shelters on actual Hamsi seed 1235 because the query tree was stale. The first alcove has a physical amber activation switch; its E prompt starts the optional pursuit. It requires at least two usable alcoves, a real reachable spawn 8–16 m from the initiator, at least 7 m from every living crewmate, with initial actual perception and floor/body clearance, no safe-zone spawn, no alive boss/cycle encounter, and no active missions14 bonus vault. Exactly one Warden may be started per landing. API: `trigger(playerId)`, `active()`, `hidden(playerId)`, `shelters()`; missions14 reciprocally guards against active pursuit.

Signal Warden is an original asymmetric tripod carrying a ceramic receiver and amber slot, not an invisible cosmetic figure. Its initial two-click warning lasts 2.2 s. Chase speed asks for 5.6 m/s (player sprint is 8.2); existing chase tuning still applies. It follows visible players, remembers only the last seen position, searches after 2.2 s without sight or after a 12 s chase, then ends the single attempt. Genuine visual contact during the warning or chase is required: a never-seen activation cannot grant a reward. The target must then stay unseen for the full six-second search; reacquiring sight returns to chase and requires a fresh six-second search after the next sight break. Total attempt time is capped at 35 seconds. Doors in its actual forward route cost 1.8 s to open; a nearby closed door behind it does not stall it. Static movement probes stop it crossing physical shelter walls even where the facility nav grid predates the new panels.

A hit requires a visible, unhidden player within 1.8 m following a separate 1.1 s metallic wind-up. Breaking sight, moving out of reach or entering shelter cancels it. Damage is capped at 32 (24 base), then the attempt fails and it retires; no instant-kill call is used. Entrance/ship safe-zone players are excluded. Stun, a connected hit, death/downing, boss overlap and an uninterrupted visible 12-second chase cannot award the recording; visible search reacquires pursuit instead. Killing it follows ordinary combat rewards and fails this escape objective. Retirement uses hostRemove and its existing removal packet, never kill processing or free kill XP.

Enter a screening alcove with E; entering forces crouch and holds movement, while mouse look stays available to watch the Warden and choose an exit. Ordinary frozen/cutscene states still hold look. The leave prompt remains directly available inside. A host-authoritative membership book enforces one crewmate per alcove, approach distance, vertical band and real LOS, and rejects downed players, revivers, trolley drivers, giant/two-hand cargo and body carrying. Giant grab ownership also blocks entry. Members can remain quiet for up to 20 s. Speaking loudly or expiration ejects them with four-second Warden rest grace. When the Warden actually witnessed the entry, it may inspect after arriving at the last seen position: a three-second warning precedes ejection, again with rest grace. Unwitnessed entries do not grant supernatural inspection knowledge. This is Warden-specific counterplay; other threats are blocked by the physical shelter as applicable, not a blanket invulnerability mode.

Host membership and single-attempt state live in `run.escape14`; e14state/e14say are host-only channels. Enter/leave/start use authenticated sender-bound e14act requests, never payload player IDs. Creature position/state/damage use existing cev/cs packets. State sync restores local movement on leaving; unload/dispose remove colliders and meshes and release the local crouch/frozen state. Host migration clears timed hiding membership instead of trusting the prior host's clock. Ordinary late-join run state carries the hiding book. `LocalPlayer` has one approved crouch hook for `this.hiding`.

Original procedural warning, wind-up, search and impact sounds share the existing positional mix; the initiating player also receives the warning directly. Generic robot state voices do not mask those cues. Actual Warden model materials are registered with the landing warm hook on eligible quota-2 facility landings, preventing first-pursuit compilation work where warmset/landingq are enabled. No warm work is added for newcomers or Company HQ. Existing footstep cadence handles its moving chase. EN/TR/RU names, lore, prompts and warnings are included. Real headphone perception is not verified by numerical sample checks.

Validation: `escape14.test.mjs` exercises actual installer/host AI, real generic creature snapshots, physical shelter construction, sender-bound requests, crew occupancy, replica membership, timeout and disposal, heavy/cart/revive refusals, boss/bonus-mission and single-use gates, warning/wind-up/LOS counterplay, closed-door delay, ignoring a door behind, physical movement obstruction, witnessed-vs-unwitnessed inspection, shader warm hook, original model animation and finite bounded audio recipes. SFX fixture now registers actual escape14 definitions; 6,894 checks pass. Broad perf5 passes: sum medians 0.557 ms/tick over 134 handlers, escape14 median 0.0015 ms/max 0.024 ms on its Node stub. These are fixture measurements, not human-tested combat balance or browser FPS. The regression additionally builds the real factory seed 1235 with actual Rapier, confirms no mapLoaded probe, steps physics and verifies two or more reachable shelter exits and actual floor hits. Shared QA must physically navigate the actual alcoves and console on a generated map.

Limits: the alcove floor/exit/nav checks do not prove clearance against every decorative prop in every interior. QA should inspect overlap, prompt visibility and real crouch camera fit. Doors can still buy permanent escape if the nav path remains disconnected; pursuit is deliberately bounded. Other existing directors may spawn ordinary threats; this module explicitly prevents its own boss/bonus-mission overlap but is not a global director rewrite.

The existing objective list shows one instruction while pursuit is active, then one collection instruction after success. At the first shelter, E claims one physical Signal Pursuit Recording (85 salvage value, 2 lb, existing VHS model). Host distance, living/downed and real LOS checks protect collection. The claim commits only after standard hostSpawn succeeds; duplicate requests cannot create another recording. It must be carried and sold through ordinary salvage rules; it does not add immediate credits or quota. Quota-0/1 shelter interaction labels remain hidden. Tests additionally cover never-seen starts, uninterrupted visible timeouts, reacquired sight during search, quiet unwitnessed shelter success, stun/boss/death/hit failures, actual removal packets, failed spawn retry and exactly one physical claim.
