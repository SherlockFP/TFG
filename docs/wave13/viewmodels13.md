# Viewmodel utility grips — wave 13

The airhorn and clown horn meshes are authored with the bell along +Z, while the camera-aligned hand frame treats -Z as forward. Their generic scrap grip also used the combined bounding box rather than the canister/bulb. `fpbody_grip.js` now classifies these two items as utility tools, rotates the bell forward and explicitly grips the canister or squeeze bulb. The Adblock extinguisher grips its bottle instead of the nozzle junction; this removes the previous roughly 9 cm palm gap.

`actions.updateViewModel` adds the existing local item-use age to its pose inputs. `models/avatar.js` uses it for a bounded 0.38-second horn lift/squeeze and torch/walkie switch click. This is independent of melee arcs and recoil, with a small displacement so the held item stays clear of the crosshair. Flashlight/proflash barrel direction is preserved; the current fit already puts the lens ahead of the glove. Existing swing, charge, mouse sway and reduced-motion pipelines remain. No dropped/world mesh is resized or changed, and weapon registration hooks remain available to arsenal13.

Validation: extended existing `ux_grip.test.mjs` with actual horn, flashlight, proflash and adblock geometry. It asserts utility classification, forward bell/lens axis, sleeve clearance, near-plane clearance and reasonable palm distance. Existing melee grip checks, n3fix head-visibility suite and feelfix2 bulky screen-coverage suite pass. The full 101-item offline geometry audit reports 0 sleeve penetrations, 0 behind-camera items, 0 off-screen items; two pre-existing palm-distance findings (Old Router and one fish) remain. Syntax checks pass.

Browser QA delegated to the coordinated playtest agent: equip horn, inspect bell direction and press LMB, advance 0.19 s for the lift peak, test flashlight toggle and adblock.
