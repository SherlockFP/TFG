# Wave18 Algorithm sorting bureau

The company exchange previously retained a broad rusty garage frontage around its improved native Archive Intake. This pass replaces that flat fascia with an asymmetric stepped sorting canopy, tall magenta authority spine and ribbed archive roofline. The architecture, not another repeated title, defines the approach silhouette.

Inside, two suspended packet lanes split preservation from rejection and frame the intake without covering its usable tray or compact receipt mechanism. Static parcels are a process snapshot, not an additional conveyor gameplay promise. The west wall has flush rejection drawers and short localized evidence plaques; the east wall's middle band becomes framed transparent archive glass, revealing the existing storage towers. Its original solid glass-wall collision remains. Obsolete fish/ice crate visuals are replaced by sealed archive cartons with the same native physical bounds and rotations. Internal item/prop IDs remain intact.

The colors follow a consistent purpose: mint for crew/content flow, amber for industry/storage, magenta for Algorithm classification authority. Four EN/TR/RU plaques explain preservation, sealed storage, rejection and the absent author. Existing intake, market, broker, casino, workshop and neutral NPC actions remain the only promised interactions.

Physical contracts are unchanged: 110 native colliders and 17 pooled emitters. No new scene light, per-frame mesh, NPC, wallet or simulation was added. The new layer contains 8 merged static batches and 1,486 triangles, including one 1024×256 localized plaque atlas. Its private geometry/materials/atlas and language subscription are explicitly released before native map cleanup; shared level textures remain cached.
